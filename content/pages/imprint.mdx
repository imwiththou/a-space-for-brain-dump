### Privacy

If you contact me, I will use the information you provide to read and respond to your message. Your email or messaging provider will also process your message under its own privacy policy.

When you visit a website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the pages requested to deliver and secure the site. External links take you to other services, which have their own privacy practices.

Singapore's [Personal Data Protection Act 2012](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012) sets out rules for the handling of personal data. Its data protection provisions do not impose obligations on an individual acting in a personal or domestic capacity. You can learn more from the [Personal Data Protection Commission](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/).
